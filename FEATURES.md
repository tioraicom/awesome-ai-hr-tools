# Features

What this repository does today, what is committed, and what is only an idea. The three
headings are not the same promise, and the difference is the point of the file.

## Available

Shipping now, in this release.

- **58 tools across 10 parts of the HR year**, grouped by when the work happens rather than by software category.
- **One stated editorial position.** A tool may prepare a judgement about a person and may not make one, applied consistently and at a cost the methodology names.
- **Two complete editions.** English and Arabic, same entries, same order, descriptions written in each language rather than translated.
- **No fairness or accuracy ranking**, because vendor bias-audit summaries cannot be reproduced from outside the product.
- **Every link resolved over HTTP** before release, and pricing and platform labels from the portfolio's shared vocabularies.

## Planned

Committed. These have a decided shape and will land; nothing is listed here to make the
repository look busier than it is.

- **A pass every quarter**, because a vendor that repositions an optional ranking feature as its product stops qualifying, and that happens between releases.
- **Entries for tools the issue tracker asks about**, which is better evidence of a gap than guessing at one.

## Ideas

Not committed, and possibly never. Listed because a contributor may want to argue for one, or
build it. Opening an issue about any of them is welcome.

- **A column naming which regulator each vendor states it has audited against.** Useful, and it would need a lawyer rather than a curator to keep honest.
- **A companion list of what was excluded and why.** Tempting, and it would be a list of named companies accused of something, which is not what a curated list is for.
- **A section for works councils and employee representatives**, who are a real reader with an opposite question. Probably a different repository.
